## Directional Semantics

The legacy `votes` object is retained only for transport compatibility. With
the current artifact, its values count outputs from one eligible directional
estimator. They are not independent votes and must not be presented as model
consensus.

The authoritative entry decision remains the canonical Python contract:

1. exact authority and artifact identities;
2. `selected`;
3. `side`;
4. `ENTER_NOW` or `DO_NOT_ENTER`;
5. original TypeScript operational and exchange-safety controls.

TypeScript must not require two directional votes, duplicate the current
estimator output, or reinterpret specialists as directional voters.

## Specialist Roles

QMAE, tail risk, regime, entry timing, reversal risk and clean-entry
probability are specialist evidence. They may support a future calibrated
meta-selector, but they are not directional votes.

A future directional ensemble may claim consensus only after separately
trained members demonstrate incremental out-of-sample information and their
error correlation is measured. Majority voting over dependent members is not
authorized.

## ExitEye V2 Shadow

ExitEye V2 observes the current position context and canonical Python
direction. It may record only:

- `KEEP_MANAGING`;
- `PROTECT_PROFIT`;
- `EXIT_RECOMMENDED`;
- `UNKNOWN`.

Its output is counterfactual evidence. It cannot close a position, move a
stop, change a callback, cancel an order, replace a bracket or affect current
position management. A Python `HOLD` is not treated as an exit instruction.

V1 of this observer does not claim persistence modeling or a validated exit
model. Those remain prerequisites for any future promotion.

## Existing Protection

The current bracket, stop-loss, take-profit, trailing, callback and original
ExitEye behavior remain unchanged. ExitEye V2 runs beside them and records
what it would have recommended.
