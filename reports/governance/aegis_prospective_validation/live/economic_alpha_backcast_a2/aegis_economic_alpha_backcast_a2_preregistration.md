# Aegis Economic Alpha Backcast A2 - Preregistration

## Purpose

A2 attempts to refute, not rescue, the two strongest descriptive clues from
A1: trend-acceptance LONG at 60 minutes and carry-convergence SHORT at 24
hours. Every feature, scale, clipping bound, eligibility threshold, score and
calibration cutoff comes unchanged from A1.

No machine-learning model is trained in A2. Its primary question is whether
the frozen A1 top-rank score adds value over the transparent eligibility rule,
eligible-random selection and simple directional controls.

## Evidence Boundary

The evaluation period is June through December 2023, with May 2023 reserved
only for causal feature warmup and January 2024 downloaded only to complete
future paths. A1 did not use these rows, but older repository experiments have
used historical data reaching 2019. This is therefore a reverse-time external
backcast, not a project-wide untouched holdout.

A2 may reject A1 or justify a new prospective forward-collection contract. It
cannot confirm Live edge or authorize modeling, Shadow or Live by itself.

## Economic Test

Entry is the next 15-minute open after a completed causal state. Outcomes use
one-minute paths, actual archived funding and round-trip cost views of 0, 8,
14 and 20 basis points. The 14-bps view is primary.

Primary events use the top frozen A1 score and at least 24 hours of spacing per
symbol. Controls include all eligible events, eligible-random,
regime-matched-random, simple momentum, simple reversal and BTC.

Passing requires at least 100 events, 42 bps gross expectancy, positive net
expectancy and uncertainty lower bounds, broad symbol participation,
stability across temporal thirds, survival at 20 bps and superiority to every
control. The rank score must specifically outperform all eligible events.

## Safety

A2 uses checksum-verified public archives only. It performs no authenticated
exchange request, has no exchange mutation authority and cannot alter PM2,
Live, Shadow, models, capital, orders or positions.
