# Aegis W13 Signal-Conditioned Micro-Path Confirmation - Preregistration

Registered before feature extraction, labels or model fitting on W13 data.

## Question

Given an immutable Aegis LONG/SHORT signal, can causal BOOK/QUOTE/TRADE and
price-path evidence observed during the following 100 ms to 30 seconds support
one terminal decision, `ENTER` or `CANCEL`, while sufficient net edge remains?
The side cannot change and reentry is prohibited.

## Binding Data Gate

- Primary unit: independent `aegis_signal_episode_id`.
- Minimum W13_TRAIN: 1,000 episodes.
- Minimum W13_VALIDATION: 500 episodes.
- Minimum per partition: 4 symbols, 3 temporal days and 1 direction.
- LONG claims require independent LONG evidence.
- Failure action: stop before features, labels, models or thresholds.

The local frozen brain replay is counterfactual. It must not be described as a
model that was contemporaneously running on the historical Tardis dates.
Only episodes generated by a model and selection policy frozen before their
signal timestamp may enter the binding no-lookahead sample gate.

## Frozen Design

- Windows: 100, 250, 500 ms; 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 30 seconds.
- Features: price path, trade flow, flow/price response, L2 dynamics and
  absorption proxies, each tested through preregistered ablations.
- Targets from each possible decision time: remaining MFE/MAE, first barrier
  and net return. Future values are labels only.
- Latency: 0, 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 ms; 250 ms must survive.
- Costs: 14 bps baseline and 20 bps stress.
- Economic hurdle: at least +2 bps net per executed trade and non-negative net
  bps per original signal.
- Bootstrap: 10,000 resamples by signal episode with temporal blocks.
- FINAL_HOLDOUT_W13: `SEALED_NOT_OPENED`.

The machine permits only `PENDING -> ENTER` or `PENDING -> CANCEL`. It has no
flip, reentry, exit optimization or execution authority.

The complete machine-readable contract is
`config/experiments/aegis_signal_conditioned_micro_path_w13.yaml`.
