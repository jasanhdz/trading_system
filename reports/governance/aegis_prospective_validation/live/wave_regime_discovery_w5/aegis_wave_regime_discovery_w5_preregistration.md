# Aegis W5 GOOD/BAD Wave Regime Preregistration

W5 is a retrospective diagnostic experiment. It does not retune W1-W3 and has
no production, Shadow or promotion authority. Its population is the frozen W1
`WAVE_CANDIDATE/A_IMMEDIATE` population.

DISCOVERY covers August-October 2025. DEVELOPMENT_VALIDATION covers
November 2025-January 2026, separated by a 180-minute purge. Both periods were
part of the old W1 TRAIN partition; therefore even a positive result would
require genuinely new confirmation. Existing W1 VALIDATION and every W1-W3
holdout remain prohibited and unread.

GOOD means +0.50 ATR occurred before -0.25 ATR within 30 minutes, net potential
exceeded 14 bps costs, MAE did not exceed 0.25 ATR, MFE/MAE was at least 1.5,
and path efficiency was at least 0.25. BAD means the adverse barrier occurred
first or terminal net return was at most -14 bps. Remaining episodes are
NEUTRAL.

Discovery methods are fixed distributions, mutual information, L2 logistic
regression, a depth-three tree and KMeans with six clusters. At most ten frozen
candidates may reach one-shot validation. A passing regime requires at least
2,000 episodes, 500 correlation clusters, +2 bps net expectancy, positive
bootstrap lower bound, survival at 20 bps costs, seven positive symbols, two
positive temporal thirds, controlled symbol concentration and FDR significance.

Exact trend age and historical support/resistance distance are not present in
the frozen W1 schema. W5 uses disclosed maturity, extension, MA99 and
consolidation proxies and does not invent those unavailable fields.
