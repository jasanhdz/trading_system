# AEGIS W11 Ephemeral Regime Alpha

Offline, disposable research sandbox for testing whether a model trained only on the
most recent market state can produce short-lived net economic edge.

## Safety Boundary

- Reads local historical public market data only.
- Does not import or modify production, TypeScript, E4, PM2, exchange, live config,
  canonical datasets, existing shadow services, or sealed holdouts.
- Writes only below this sandbox.
- An expired model instance is immutable and can never reactivate.

## Frozen Study

- Source: 11-symbol Binance futures 1-minute candles from May-December 2023.
- Causal bars: completed 5-minute bars; decisions every 15 minutes.
- Recent training windows: 6h, 12h, 24h, 48h, 72h.
- Forward targets: 5m, 15m, 30m, 60m.
- Costs: 14 bps baseline, 20 bps stress, 30 bps severe stress.
- Final prospective interval: `[2023-11-01, 2024-01-01)`.

See `w11_data_audit.md` and `w11_preregistration.md` before interpreting results.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=sandbox/aegis_ephemeral_regime_w11/src \
  .venv/bin/python -m aegis_ephemeral_regime_w11.cli audit

PYTHONPATH=sandbox/aegis_ephemeral_regime_w11/src \
  .venv/bin/python -m aegis_ephemeral_regime_w11.cli run

PYTHONPATH=sandbox/aegis_ephemeral_regime_w11/src \
  .venv/bin/pytest sandbox/aegis_ephemeral_regime_w11/tests
```

Generated machine-readable outputs live in `artifacts/`. The final human report is
`w11_ephemeral_regime_result.md`; the verdict is
`w11_ephemeral_regime_verdict.json`.

## Final Result

Status: **CLOSED - NO POSITIVE PROSPECTIVE ECONOMIC REPRODUCTION**

Preregistered classification:

`B - EPHEMERAL_SIGNAL_DETECTED_NOT_YET_ECONOMIC`

The procedure found local candidates during forward validation, but their apparent
edge did not survive the prospective period from November through December 2023.
This experiment does not justify Shadow, production integration, or further E4/TS
architecture work.

Primary prospective evidence:

- 100 immutable ERE instances and 1,922 attributed trades across 11 symbols.
- Mean gross return: `-1.83 bps` per trade.
- Mean net return: `-15.83 bps` at 14-bps cost, `-21.83 bps` at 20-bps stress,
  and `-31.83 bps` at 30-bps severe stress.
- Synchronized UTC-day bootstrap 95% interval at baseline cost:
  `[-23.39, -9.43] bps`; probability of positive mean: `0%`.
- The system remained SKIP for `97.02%` of prospective symbol snapshots.
- 94 instances expired through edge decay and 6 through regime drift; none reached
  TTL before another expiration mechanism.
- Regime similarity did not predict future edge (`Spearman = -0.017`).
- The Guardian reduced aggregate exposure and drawdown by stopping losing experts,
  but did not improve per-trade expectancy.

Descriptive subgroups were also not economic. The least-negative window was 48h
(`+6.12 bps` gross, `-7.88 bps` net at baseline cost), and the least-negative
horizon was 30m (`+9.28 bps` gross, `-4.72 bps` net). These are post-run
diagnostics, not selection authority or evidence for retuning.

The always-skip baseline remained economically superior. Always-long and 15-minute
mean reversion also lost after costs, but lost less per trade than ERE. There is no
system-level edge half-life to estimate because aggregate initial net edge was
already negative; isolated initially positive instances decayed with a descriptive
median of roughly 3h.

## Closure Decision

W11 is retained as negative evidence to prevent repeated rediscovery and parameter
mining. Do not reopen the experiment by changing windows, symbols, sides, horizons,
costs, or activation thresholds against the same prospective interval. Any future
attempt must be a newly preregistered experiment with new independent evidence and a
new model family/version. Expired W11 instances remain expired forever.
